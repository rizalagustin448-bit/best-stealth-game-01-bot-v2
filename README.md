Beaconkeeper: The Midnight Light — a top-down stealth heist in a lighthouse tower: a rotating beam sweeps the Lamp Room, lantern keepers patrol with raycast vision cones and suspicion rings, fog banks and crates hide the thief, pebbles and the hall bell lure guards away, and three floors (the Boathouse, the Keeper's Quarters, the Lamp Room) ask you to steal the Tide Chart, the Cipher Wheel and the Keeper's Logbook — then reach the exit unseen. Walk is quick but audible, Shift is a slow silent sneak, standing in lamplight or the beam gets you spotted fast, and a ghost run (never seen) pays a bonus. WebAudio footsteps, bell tolls and alarm stings; title, briefing, pause, floor-clear and win screens included.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Move with WASD or arrow keys, hold Shift to sneak, press F or click to throw a pebble, E to ring the bell near it, P to pause, M to mute. Screenshots: `?shot=cover`, `?shot=dock`, `?shot=chase`, `?shot=win`.
